# Bank Contact Guide — Working Capital

## Truist Bank

**Small Business Lending:**
- Phone: 833-866-5129 (Small Business Specialist)
- Schedule appointment: https://www.truist.com/small-business
- Locations: https://www.truist.com/locations

**Products to ask about:**
- Business Line of Credit (unsecured, up to $250K)
- Business Term Loan
- SBA 7(a) Loan
- Equipment Financing

**What they'll want:**
- 2+ years in business (may be flexible)
- $100K+ annual revenue
- 670+ credit score (typical)
- Business tax returns, financials, bank statements

**Your angle:** Emphasize the SBA options — Truist is an SBA Preferred Lender. SBA loans have more flexible credit requirements.

**⚠️ Skip unless SBA specialist is exceptional — user hates Truist bureaucracy**

## Alternative Options (Better for 550 Credit)

### SBA Microloan Program
- Up to $50K
- Credit requirements more flexible
- Find lenders: https://www.sba.gov/funding-programs/loans/microloans

### CDFIs (Community Development Financial Institutions)
- Mission-driven lenders
- Work with lower credit scores
- Find one: https://www.cdfifund.gov/cdfis

### NC Capital Access Program
- NC-specific program
- Helps banks lend to businesses that don't meet traditional criteria
- https://www.nccommerce.com/grants-incentives/capital-access

### Equipment Financing Companies
- If buying vehicles/equipment, the asset is collateral
- Credit requirements lower
- Examples: Balboa Capital, National Equipment Finance

### Revenue-Based Financing
- If OmniTender has processing revenue
- Repayment tied to revenue
- Examples: Pipe, Lighter Capital (for tech companies)
